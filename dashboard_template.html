Problem and objective

Card and wallet fraud costs money and trust, but blocking too aggressively loses good customers. The goal is to catch most fraud while keeping false declines and analyst workload manageable.

Success measures

  • Fraud catch rate (recall) of 85% or more
  • Precision of 40% or more among flagged transactions
  • Under 8% of transactions sent to manual review
  • Decision in under 200 ms

Stakeholders

  • Fraud operations: work the review queue, need clear reasons
  • Customers: want fewer wrongful declines
  • Finance: tracks fraud loss and chargebacks
  • Compliance: audit trail, PCI DSS and data privacy
  • Product: checkout friction

Requirements (MoSCoW)

  • Must: score every transaction in real time; explain each score; approve, review or block bands
  • Must: adjustable thresholds with cost impact shown
  • Should: per-customer baseline; analyst feedback loop
  • Could: device graph; step-up authentication
  • Won't (v1): automated chargeback disputes

Decision process

Transaction→Feature checks→Risk score 0–100→Approve / Review / Block→Analyst label→Threshold review

Assumptions

  • Data is synthetic (about 3% fraud) and scored by the trained model, so real rates will differ
  • Review is assumed to resolve correctly
  • Block friction ₹150 and review time ₹60 per case are placeholders to validate with Finance
